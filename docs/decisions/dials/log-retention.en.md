# Log Retention Period

!!! abstract "TL;DR"
    Control how long agent logs and traces are retained, balancing compliance against storage costs.

## Overview

An audit arrives requesting the rationale behind an agent decision from six months ago, but logs were deleted after three months -- in regulated industries, this constitutes a compliance violation. Conversely, retaining everything indefinitely has led to storage costs ballooning to hundreds of thousands of yen per month.

This dial determines the retention period for logs generated by agents (execution traces, input/output records, audit logs). Logs are divided between short-term fast storage (Hot) and long-term cheap storage (Cold), allocated based on data freshness and access frequency. In regulated industries, minimum retention periods are externally mandated.

## Why Adjustment Is Needed

Agent logs include the full input/output text of LLMs, making them orders of magnitude larger than traditional application logs. Retaining everything on fast storage over the long term causes cost explosion. Meanwhile, in regulated industries, premature deletion of logs constitutes a compliance violation. Without retention period design, you fall into either "cost overruns" or "audit non-compliance."

## Extremes of the Range

### Too Small

Regulatory retention period requirements cannot be met, and logs cannot be produced during audits or litigation. Past incident root cause analysis cannot be performed, preventing remediation measures. Long-term trend analysis for quality improvement also becomes impossible.

### Too Large

Storage costs become enormous. Logs including LLM input/output run several KB to several MB per request and can reach tens of TB annually. Long-term retention of logs containing personal data also risks violating GDPR's "data minimization principle."

## Determining Forces

- `[F8]` Accountability / Regulation -- Industry regulations determine minimum retention periods, and data protection regulations constrain maximum retention periods
- `[F7]` Cost Sensitivity / Scale -- The product of log volume and storage unit cost directly determines cost

## Guidelines (Starting Point)

- Hot (fast, searchable): 7-30 days
- Warm (retrievable within hours): 30-90 days
- Cold (archive): 1-7 years (depending on regulatory requirements)
- Logs containing personal data: Align with regulatory required periods; anonymize or delete beyond that
- Audit logs (who approved what, and when): Longest retention

## Practical Adjustment

- Confirm regulatory requirements (financial: 5-7 years, healthcare: 6-10 years, etc.) with legal and determine minimum retention periods
- Implement Hot/Warm/Cold tiering with lifecycle policies that automatically migrate data
- Apply masking or anonymization to logs containing personal data so retention periods can be shortened
- Visualize storage costs monthly and review the relationship between retention periods and costs
